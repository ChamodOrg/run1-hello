# Greeting

## Purpose

A visitor opens the app and is shown a hello message.

## User Stories

- F1.1 As a visitor, I open the app and see the greeting "Hello, World!".
- F1.2 As a visitor, I see the greeting without signing in.

## Decisions

- The greeting text is "Hello, World!".
- The page is view only; the visitor has nothing to click or enter.

## Out of Scope

- Buttons, refresh or re-greet interactions.
- Personalised names and language choice.
